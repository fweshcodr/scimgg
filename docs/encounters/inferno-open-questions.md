# Inferno: open questions (waves 1–68)

What the captures do not explain, where sources disagree, and what the simulator does without
direct evidence, at the level of mechanics. Facts and capture citations live in the
[facts spec](./inferno-facts-spec.md); the simulator's choices in the [policy](./inferno-policy.md);
replay findings and the coverage ledger in [capture replay](../testing/capture-timeline.md#findings).
The exhaustive list of declared replay divergences is the conformance suite,
`src/encounters/inferno/__tests__/captureConformance.test.ts`. Wave 69 has its own list:
[`zuk-open-questions.md`](./zuk-open-questions.md).

Sources as in the facts spec: **P**, **Q** (captures; `R:t` = P's resumed wave-68 stream), **W** (OSRS
Wiki). Replay status is the classification in `captureConformance.test.ts`: *expected* (declared
divergence), *inconclusive* (follows a free draw or an earlier divergence), *conformant*.
